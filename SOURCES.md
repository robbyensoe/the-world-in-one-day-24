# Sources — the stories on the page

All stories happened, or were first reported, on Tuesday 24 November 2026
(the market hero is Tuesday's story: Monday's record rebound and its
Tuesday fade, reported as one arc by Tuesday's market press).

- **Global markets / Nvidia** — Nvidia surged 31% on Monday, its largest
  one-day gain since 2003, adding nearly $1 trillion in market value after
  the board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO following pressure from
  President Trump (KuCoin daily market report, 24 Nov; Investopedia,
  24 Nov). Monday closes: S&P 500 +4.73% to 7,237, Nasdaq +6.51%, Nikkei
  225 +3.91% (Horizon market wrap, 24 Nov). On Tuesday the rally faded:
  Nvidia fell ~5% premarket and Bitcoin dropped below $60,000 after
  touching $64,500, with gold easing to $3,622/oz (CoinDesk, 24 Nov).
- **Libya / Ukraine** — Ukraine insisted its sinking of the Russian
  corvette Merkury in Benghazi port was self-defence under Article 51 of
  the UN Charter; Tripoli's government condemned the operation and the UN
  mission sought a probe into the Benghazi port attacks (Reuters, 24 Nov;
  WSJ inside account, 24 Nov; UN News via The Global Eye, 24 Nov). The
  strike itself, by seven naval drones, was claimed by President Zelensky
  on 23 Nov.
- **Space / Tiangong** — Pakistan's Zeeshan Ali will become the first
  foreign astronaut aboard China's Tiangong station, flying on the
  Shenzhou-24 mission (The Straits Times, via Horizon and OneTurn news
  summaries, 24 Nov).
- **Indonesia** — authorities confirmed on Tuesday that 26 workers were
  killed in the Cikande candy-factory fire in Banten, most of them found
  on the second floor trying to reach an emergency exit; 26 body bags
  recovered, eight workers still missing, more than 600 rescuers on site
  (Xinhua, 24 Nov; The Straits Times, 24 Nov).
- **NATO / space security** — NATO chief Mark Rutte said the alliance
  will defend against any threats in space after reports that Russia is
  preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite (BBC, via The Global Eye,
  24 Nov).
- **Gaza** — Israeli airstrikes killed at least six Palestinians,
  including two police officers, in the Gaza Strip on Tuesday (Reuters,
  via Al Arabiya, 24 Nov); a UN report the same day said the Gaza war has
  undone 77 years of human development (UN, via Principia, 24 Nov).
- **Pharma** — France's Sanofi agreed to buy US biotech Insmed for
  $41 billion, the largest biotech deal in nearly three years (Principia
  business summary, 24 Nov).
- **Sport** — Champions League, Tuesday night: Atlético Madrid beat
  Manchester United 2-0, Club Brugge won 4-3 at Inter Milan, and Bayern
  Munich were held 3-3 by Viking Stavanger (Africa Sport Network match
  round-up, 24 Nov).
- **In brief** — the United States lost its measles elimination status
  after more than 25 years (Principia, 24 Nov); the FDA approved the
  first drug for limb-girdle muscular dystrophy, from BridgeBio
  (Principia, 24 Nov); a magnitude 5.6 earthquake struck Nepal's Koshi
  province near the Chinese border (Horizon, 24 Nov); Germany became the
  first country granted access to Ukraine's Delta battlefield management
  system, under a letter of intent signed in Berlin on 24 Nov (The Kyiv
  Independent, 24 Nov).
